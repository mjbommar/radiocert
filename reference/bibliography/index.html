  • Amateur Radio Question Pools (Elements 2, 3, and 4)

    pinned

    ncvec-pools · National Conference of Volunteer Examiner Coordinators · standard · retrieved 2026-07-30

    Supports

    • •Every question, keyed answer, and syllabus group in the three amateur tracks

    Released publicly because 47 CFR 97.523 requires it. For what the exam asks, this is the only authority — including where its keyed answer simplifies or lags the regulation, which the lessons say out loud.

Level 3 — Official agency guidance

Official agency guidance: public notices, datasets, accepted standards.

    This is the link between the performance rule in Part 89 and the radio layer in ASTM F3411 — the reason Part 89 never names a frequency.

  • OET Bulletin 65 Supplement B — Additional Information for Amateur Radio Stations

    pinned

    fcc-oet65b · FCC Office of Engineering and Technology · guidance · retrieved 2026-07-30

    Supports

    • •G0A06 names this bulletin by title as the way a station evaluation is performed
    • •The station-evaluation tables behind 'perform a routine RF exposure evaluation'

    Predates the 2019 and 2021 rule changes that removed the categorical power exemptions, so its *procedure* remains current while its exemption thresholds do not. Lessons cite it for method, never for who is exempt.

    rf-exposuresafetympeamateurevaluation

  • PRB-1 — Amateur Radio Preemption, 101 FCC 2d 952 (1985)

    fcc-prb-1 · Federal Communications Commission · notice · retrieved 2026-07-30

    Supports

    • •PRB-1 is a 1985 Memorandum Opinion and Order reported at 101 FCC 2d 952

    Rarely needs to be read directly: 47 CFR 97.15(b) codifies the holding and cites the docket by name, so the rule text is both the better citation and the one already pinned in this build.
