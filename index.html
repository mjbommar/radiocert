Radio & Electronics Foundations

The physics, math, and circuit vocabulary every radio credential assumes. Built so that a lesson in any exam track can point here instead of re-teaching Ohm's law for the fourth time.

14 lessons

Amateur Radio — Technician Class

26/35 to pass

FCC Element 2. Entry-level US amateur licence: all amateur privileges above 30 MHz plus limited HF. Gateway credential for the VHF/UHF gear already on the bench.

26 lessonspool tech-2026

Amateur Radio — General Class

26/35 to pass

FCC Element 3. Opens the majority of HF: worldwide voice, digital, and CW operation, with the propagation and antenna theory that go with it.

35 lessonspool gen-2023pool expires in 331 days

Amateur Radio — Amateur Extra Class

37/50 to pass

FCC Element 4. Full US amateur privileges. The pool is the deepest technical material in the amateur service: transmission lines, Smith charts, digital signal processing, complex impedance, and test equipment.

51 lessonspool extra-2024

GMRS — General Mobile Radio Service

No exam, but real rules. Channel plan, power and antenna limits, repeater use, identification, and the FRS/GMRS/MURS boundary that trips up most operators using the handhelds already in the drawer.

5 lessons

SDR & Bench Practicum

Hands-on labs that turn exam abstractions into things you have seen on a screen: spectrum, modulation, filters, antenna resonance, propagation. Every lab names the hardware it needs and offers a no-hardware fallback.

8 lessons

FAA Part 107 — Radio, Airspace & Spectrum

Not a from-scratch Part 107 course — the certificate is already held. This track covers the recurrent-training knowledge areas and the places where drone operations meet radio: airspace and ATC comms, LAANC, aircraft band monitoring, C2 link spectrum, and Remote ID.

5 lessons

Where to start

  1. Foundations — the electrical vocabulary every other track assumes. Skip it only if Ohm's law and decibels are already automatic.
  2. A diagnostic practice exam — 35 questions drawn in the real subelement proportions tells you which modules you can skim.
  3. The knowledge graph — when a lesson references something you do not hold yet, follow the prerequisite edges backwards.

Study material derived from primary sources: NCVEC question pools published under 47 CFR 97.523, and regulation text from eCFR. Nothing here is legal advice or an official FCC or FAA publication.
